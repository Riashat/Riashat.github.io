I am a Senior Research Scientist at Microsoft Research. Previously, I was a PhD student supervised by Doina Precup in the Reasoning and Learning Lab at McGill University, part of Mila – Quebec AI Institute, while also working as a Student Researcher at Microsoft Research NYC and Montreal with John Langford, Alex Lamb, Dipendra Misra, Remi Tachet Des Combes, Romain Laroche, and Harm Van Seijen. I also collaborate closely with Anirudh Goyal and Yoshua Bengio. For past and present collaborators, see the full list here.

I completed my Masters at the University of Cambridge in the MPhil Machine Learning, Speech and Language Technology Program, supervised by Zoubin Ghahramani and Yarin Gal in the Cambridge Machine Learning Group. Prior to that, I studied Electronic and Electrical Engineering at University College London, working with John Shawe-Taylor and Guy Lever, and had the fortune of working with David Silver at Google DeepMind during my undergraduate thesis.

My research centers on reinforcement learning, representation learning, and world models — including latent state discovery, offline RL, and building compact, plannable representations for control and reasoning.
